When your team is preparing a demand package or getting ready for mediation, sorting through fragmented provider statements, CMS-1500/UB-04 forms, and unreconciled EOBs can take hours away from core legal work.

At Medical Billing Miami Beach (MBMB), we coordinate with our U.S. AAPC-certified medical coding and billing team—using human coding review plus an AI secondary check—to prepare structured administrative billing summaries for legal teams:

1. Source-Linked Medical Billing Chronology: Provider bills, EOBs, and payment records organized by date of service, provider, and code—linked to the exact source document and page number.

2. Plain-English Code Explanations: Clear explanations of CPT, ICD-10, and HCPCS codes plus billing modifiers so your team can review complex ledgers faster.

3. Charge-to-Balance Reconciliation: Structured tables separating billed charges, contractual adjustments, payments, write-offs, and open balances—while flagging duplicate entries or missing date ranges.
